Our Story

A family-run corner of the Toon since 2015

The Quayside Bean started life as a market stall under the Tyne Bridge, serving coffee to dockers and early commuters crossing from Gateshead. Ten years on, we've got a proper roastery in Ouseburn, a kitchen turning out stotties and sausage rolls, and the same stall still runs on a Saturday.

We roast in small batches, buy directly from growers in Colombia and Ethiopia where we can, and source our milk from a dairy in rural Northumberland. Everything else — the bakes, the soups, the bacon — comes from suppliers within thirty miles of the shop.
